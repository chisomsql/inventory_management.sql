# inventory_management.sql
**What can a simple SQL analysis tell us about a business?**

I recently worked on an Inventory Management SQL project where I used SQL to explore product and sales data and understand what was happening in the business.

Rather than just writing queries and looking at numbers, I wanted to understand the story behind the data.

Here are some of the things I discovered:

📌 The business generated a total revenue of **$2,230** from 4 sales transactions.

📌 A total of **11 product units** were sold.

📌 Smartphones generated the highest revenue of $1,500, making them the biggest revenue contributor in the dataset.

📌 Jeans recorded the lowest revenue at $80.

📌 The average product price was $207.50.

📌 Each of the four product categories had only one product listed, which gave me an opportunity to think about product variety and inventory management.

**What did I learn from this project?**

One thing I'm beginning to understand as I grow in data analytics is that getting results from a query is only one part of the job. Understanding what those results mean and how they can help a business make better decisions is equally important.

For this project, I practised:

- SELECT and WHERE statements
- Aggregate functions (SUM, AVG, COUNT, MAX and MIN)
- JOIN operations
- GROUP BY
- Subqueries

I'm gradually improving my SQL skills and learning to approach data with a business mindset.

Every project is another opportunity to learn, practise and improve.
